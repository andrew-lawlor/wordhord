# Art

`baade-sagatid.jpg`: Knud Baade (1808–1879), *Fantasibilde fra den norske sagatid* ("Scene from the Era of Norwegian Sagas"), 1850. Oil on canvas, Nordnorsk Kunstmuseum, Tromsø. The painting is in the public domain. The photograph is by Wikimedia Commons user Illustratedjc, CC BY-SA 3.0: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Baade-ScenefromtheEraofNorwegianSagas.jpg>.

`sleep.png`: Wordhord's sleep screen, made from it by `scripts/sleep-screen.py`: the warden on the sea-cliff, cropped to the screen, with WORDHORD set in Junicode over the sky, dithered to sixteen greys. In *Beowulf* the coast-guard on the cliffs challenges the Geats, and Beowulf, answering him, *wordhord onleac*: unlocked his word-hoard (line 259).
